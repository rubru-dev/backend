## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor of modern Indonesian architectural engineering blended with bespoke interior craftsmanship and artisan steel fabrication. The visual language conveys high-order spatial discipline, structural permanence, and material precision. 

The aesthetic is anchored in an editorial architectural catalog style:
- **Pristine Canvas & Negative Space:** 75% pure white surfaces create breathing room reminiscent of daylight-flooded open floorplans and gallery atriums.
- **Architectural Tactility:** 15% raw neutral finishes and structural photography celebrate polished terrazzo, brushed tropical teak, and powder-coated matte black steel.
- **Controlled Kinetic Energy:** 10% purposeful terracotta and industrial orange accents function as precision focal points—reminiscent of safety marks, structural welds, and surveyor pins.
- **Atmosphere:** Rigorous, warm, understated, and authoritative.

## Colors

The color palette is strictly balanced across pure structural white, soft architectural substrate tones, and high-visibility fabrication accents.

### Palette Application Rules
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Dominates 75% of viewports. Serves as the primary ground for content, full-bleed spatial showcases, and clear grid layouts.
- **Secondary Surface (`#F8F8F6`):** Applied to alternate section backdrops, elevated structural pods, and drawer panels to define architectural zoning without heavy separation lines.
- **Tertiary Surface / Warm Gray (`#F2F2EF`):** Reserved for interior framing, inactive component tracks, filter chip containers, and subtle architectural inset panels.
- **Dividers & Structural Hairlines (`#E9E9E6`):** Used consistently for micro-borders, 1px blueprint-style separation rules, and table grids.
- **Primary Accent Orange (`#F36B21`):** Applied surgically (maximum 10% visual surface) strictly for high-priority interactive buttons, active state indicators, project categorization tags, and measurement badges.
- **Deep Accent Orange (`#D9531E`):** Hover and pressed states for primary buttons, critical project milestone tags, and key architectural metrics.
- **Deep Charcoal / Structural Black (`#1A1918`):** The primary text color for headlines and technical copy to preserve high contrast and uncompromising legibility without harsh absolute black.

## Typography

The typography system relies on **Sora** for structural elements and headers paired with **Manrope** for analytical and body texts.

### Hierarchical Standards
- **Hero & Display (`64px` / `44px` mobile):** Set tight in Sora Bold with negative letter spacing, emulating architectural project folios and site signage.
- **Section Headers (`40px` / `30px` mobile):** Clear, objective, and restrained, emphasizing materiality and spatial scope.
- **Body (`15px` - `17px`):** Manrope provides geometric stability and open apertures, yielding high legibility across construction specifications, design briefs, and project logs.
- **Micro-labels & Eyebrows (`12px`):** Set in full uppercase with `0.08em` tracking to designate architectural zones, steel grades, project codes, and fabrication specs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column architectural grid inspired by modular building proportions.

### Grid Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `4rem` outer canvas margins, maintaining expansive whitespace framing around content blocks and full-height architectural renders. Max container width is fixed at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Multi-column editorial cards collapse into clean 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` side margins. Structural project blueprints, material matrices, and image spreads shift to single-column vertical flows.

### Spacing Guidelines
Component gaps follow an 8pt structural rhythm. Inner padding within architectural cards scales from `space-lg` to `space-xl` to ensure spatial elements never feel congested.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy commercial drop shadows, instead utilizing layered surface planes, razor-thin structural outlines, and diffused ambient lighting that mimics natural diffused skylights.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Foundation):** Plain `#FFFFFF` surface plane without shadow or border.
- **Level 1 (Substrate Cards & Panels):** `#F8F8F6` or `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by a 1px `#E9E9E6` perimeter border. Zero hard shadow.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Works):** Subtle spatial suspension using ambient diffused shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.06), 0 0 0 1px #E9E9E6`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Fabrication Spec Flyouts, Floating Menus):** High-diffusion overhead light shadow: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(233, 233, 230, 0.8)` complemented by a soft frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

The shape system introduces calibrated organic rounding to balance the austere, geometric lines of steel and concrete architecture.

### Specific Radii Allocations
- **Architectural Cards & Spec Pods:** `18px` (falling within the prescribed 16–22px threshold) to produce refined modern containers.
- **Hero Imagery, Spatial Galleries & Workshop Photography:** `24px` (within the 20–28px range), giving large media a smooth, gallery-curated feel.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** `12px` (strictly 10–14px range) for ergonomic tap targets that maintain precision corners.
- **Status Tags, Spec Badges & Counter Chips:** `8px` for compact, blueprint-style notation blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#F36B21` fill with crisp white text (`Sora`, 14px SemiBold), height 48px, horizontal padding 24px, border-radius 12px. Transitions to `#D9531E` on hover.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with 1px `#1A1918` border, `#1A1918` text. Inverts to solid `#1A1918` with `#FFFFFF` text on hover.
- **Architectural Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, `#1A1918` text, minimal 1px `#E9E9E6` border with hover shift to `#F8F8F6` fill.

### Tags & Chips
- **Category Badge:** `#F2F2EF` background, `#1A1918` text, radius 8px, padding 6px 12px, font size 12px.
- **Active / Milestone Indicator:** White background, 1px `#F36B21` border, `#F36B21` text, accompanied by a 6px circular `#F36B21` solid pulse dot.

### Architectural Cards
- Default card container sits on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E9E9E6` hairline border and `18px` corner radius.
- Imagery inside cards maintains a `14px` internal radius with an inset 16px padding relative to the card perimeter.
- Hover states trigger a subtle upward translation of `-4px` accompanied by the Level 2 diffused ambient shadow.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 50px, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E9E9E6` border, `12px` border radius, padding 0 16px. Active/focused border transitions to 1.5px `#F36B21` with zero fuzzy focus rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px structural square or circle with 1.5px `#767571` border. Checked state transitions to solid `#F36B21` with an architectural white checkmark or center pin.

### Minimalist Outline Iconography
- Icons are drawn on a strict 24x24px grid with a uniform 1.5px stroke, no fill, and square join terminals to reflect drafting blueprints. Accented states employ `#F36B21` stroke color.
